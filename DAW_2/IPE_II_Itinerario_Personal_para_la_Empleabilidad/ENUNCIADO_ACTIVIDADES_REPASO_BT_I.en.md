1. Which of the following is a characteristic of mobile apps for job hunting?
   - a) They are more expensive.
   - *b) They let you look for work from anywhere.
   - c) They offer fewer options than websites.
   - d) They are not available for all devices.
2. What percentage of job offers belong to the hidden job market?
   - a) 50%
   - b) 60%
   - *c) 70%
   - d) 80%
3. Which tool is key for finding job opportunities that are not visible?
   - a) Social networks.
   - b) Mobile apps.
   - *c) Networking.
   - d) Search engines.
4. Which of the following is one of the employment trends mentioned by the World Economic Forum?
   - a) A decrease in digital jobs.
   - b) Lower demand for soft skills.
   - *c) An increase in pop-up offices.
   - d) A reduction in digital transformation.
5. Which social network is mentioned as key for job hunting and networking?
   - a) Facebook.
   - b) X.
   - *c) LinkedIn.
   - d) Instagram.
6. What is the main purpose of a curriculum vitae?
   - a) To make a list of personal achievements.
   - b) To detail academic experience only.
   - *c) To present clearly and concisely the competences for a specific position.
   - d) To create a network of professional contacts.
7. Which type of CV is most suitable for someone changing employment sector who wants to highlight their competences?
   - a) Chronological.
   - *b) Functional.
   - c) Combined.
   - d) Creative.
8. What is the recommended structure for a cover letter?

9. Which of the following is one of the main goals of building a personal brand on social networks?
   - a) Sharing academic achievements only.
   - b) Increasing the number of followers quickly.
   - *c) Establishing professional relationships and creating job opportunities.
   - d) Keeping profiles completely private.
10. What steps should you take if you decide to submit a speculative application to a company?
    - a) Send a generic CV to every company.
    - *b) Research the company and personalise the application.
    - c) Wait for the company to publish an offer.
    - d) Make contact only through social networks.

## UNIT 3

### Review test (page 56 of the textbook)

1. What kind of skills are soft skills?
   - *a) Specific technical skills.
   - b) Programming skills.
   - c) Interpersonal, social and emotional skills.
   - d) Physical skills.

   - a) Punctuality.
   - *b) Resilience.
   - c) Physical dexterity.
   - d) Software knowledge.
3. An increase in unemployment rates in the tourism sector constitutes, for the SWOT matrix:
   - a) A weakness.
   - b) A strength.
   - *c) A threat.
   - d) An opportunity.
4. What is one aim of active listening?
   - a) Interrupting the other speaker.
   - b) Showing disinterest.
   - *c) Understanding and processing the other speaker's message.

   - b) Pomodoro.

9. Which of the following is an action role in a team according to Belbin?
   - a) Resource Investigator.
   - *b) Implementer.
   - c) Teamworker.
   - d) Monitor Evaluator.
