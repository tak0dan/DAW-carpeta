# Exercise statement – Internship and Self-knowledge

## Context

You want to apply to do your FCT (workplace training) internship at a company in the technology sector. The chosen company is **Artic Digital**, which specialises in the design and development of custom web applications and in UX/UI.

Website: https://articdigital.com

## Aim of the exercise

- Get to know yourself better and highlight your most important qualities.
- Write professional documentation (cover letter and CV) based on a prior analysis.
- Learn to identify how your competences align with the needs of a technology company.

## Part 1: Personal SWOT matrix

Before writing the letter and the CV, carry out a SWOT analysis of yourself in order to know yourself better and be able to highlight your qualities:

1. **Strengths (S):** skills, technical knowledge, personal competences.
2. **Weaknesses (W):** aspects to improve, technical or personal limitations.
3. **Opportunities (O):** possibilities for learning, internships, networking, sector trends.
4. **Threats (T):** competition, technological change, language requirements or other in-demand skills.

Present the SWOT matrix clearly in a 2x2 table, with at least 4 items in each cell.

This matrix will serve as the basis for highlighting your strong points in the cover letter and in the CV.

## Part 2: Cover letter

Write a formal cover letter applying for your internship, taking into account the results of your SWOT matrix. Answer these questions:

- Who are you and what are you studying?
- What can you do (technologies, languages, projects done in class)?
- Why are you interested in this company in particular?
- What personal qualities would you bring if you worked with them?
- What do you offer them and what do you expect from the internship?

Structure the letter as follows:

1. Header
2. Greeting
3. Introduction
4. Body
5. Closing
6. Sign-off

## Part 3: Curriculum Vitae (CV)

Prepare a professional CV highlighting your professional objective (doing the internship) as well as your education, experience, and technical and personal competences.
